Privacy policy

The short version: the PiTunes app collects nothing. It talks to the server you run yourself, and to nobody else on our behalf.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

What we collect

Nothing. The developer of PiTunes receives no data from the app or from your server.

  • There are no analytics and no tracking.
  • There are no ads.
  • There is no PiTunes account. You never sign up with us or give us an email address.
  • The app contains no third-party tracking or advertising code.

What the app connects to

The web app is served by your own PiTunes server, on your own hardware, and talks to that server. The optional native iPhone app connects to the server address you type in. Searches, playback, likes, playlists, listening history and podcast subscriptions go to that server and nowhere else.

Two exceptions, both of which are part of features you can see:

  • Podcast episodes play directly from the podcast publisher's own servers, the same way any podcast app plays them. The publisher can see that an episode was requested and from which IP address.
  • Sound search ("Identify a song playing nearby", in the native iPhone app only) uses Apple's ShazamKit. When you tap it, the app listens through the microphone for a few seconds. The audio is turned into a fingerprint on your phone, and Apple matches that fingerprint against its catalogue. The recording itself is not stored by the app and is not sent to your PiTunes server. Apple's handling of that request is covered by Apple's privacy policy. The app asks for microphone permission the first time, and only listens while you are using this feature.

What is stored on your device

The app stores your chosen profile, your recent searches, a few preferences and any songs you download for offline use. In the web app this lives in your browser's storage for your server's address and is removed when you clear that site's data or remove the app from your home screen. In the native iPhone app it is removed when you delete the app.

What your own server does

The PiTunes server is software you install on your own device, such as a Raspberry Pi. You control it, and we have no access to it.

  • It stores likes, playlists, listening history, pinned songs, followed artists, podcast subscriptions and listening positions for each profile, in a database on that device.
  • It keeps copies of recently played songs on that device so they start faster.
  • To do its job it makes requests from your home internet connection to YouTube (search, browsing and audio), to podcast feeds, to Apple's podcast directory (charts and search), and to image servers for artwork. Those services see the request and your home IP address, as they would for any visitor. The server does not sign in to any of them.
  • If you use Tailscale, the server asks the Tailscale software on the same device which Tailscale user a phone belongs to, so that each person gets their own profile. It stores that user's Tailscale ID next to their profile.

To erase this data, delete the server's data folder or remove the software from your device.

This website

pitunes.com is a static site with no analytics, no cookies and no tracking scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub, like any web host, may log visitors' IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

PiTunes is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
